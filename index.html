Technoblade: Origins

Chapter One: The Violin [1]

In elementary school, Alex chose to learn the violin, one of the hardest instruments to master. He struggled to learn at the beginning which resulted in him dropping to the catch up class. Then to help, his parents altered his routine, so he could practice daily for at least 15 minutes.

At the beginning of practice, his mom would use flashcards, which later Alex admitted he only tolerated because it got him out of approximately 3 minutes of real practice. With consistency, his playing picked up rather quickly and as a result he went back into the regular class after a few weeks.

After a few months of continued practice, Alex found out he was actually pretty talented at playing the violin and actually quite enjoyed it. Naturally, he joined the first violin group. At the end of the school year concert, he won a Most Improved award. When asked by his mom what lesson he had learned from this experience, it wasn’t the expected “hard work leads to success”. Instead Alex answered: “First do really badly, then do well, that impresses people!”.

Chapter Two: Ranger's Apprentice [2]

His mom had bought him a book series named Ranger’s Apprentice from John Flanagan. The series was about a young boy who wanted to be a knight but was turned down due to his small sized body and gets recruited as a ranger instead. The young boy had a horse companion which would throw remarks at his owner at any given situation. The narrative of the book combined with its humor got Alex hooked. He was obsessed and would wait eagerly for each new release.

After about a year and a half of reading, Alex found an online ad(which turned out to be real) about a ranger camp for youngsters. Luckily he was offered a spot. After arriving on camp Alex volunteered to participate in a mock duel with padded swords, he and the supervisor dueled for about 3 minutes, even though they were supposed to do it for only 10 seconds.

Children at the camp were given capes and had sneaking up lessons. His mom speculates that this camping experience impacted his Minecraft playing style later on.
