Dear reader,

You are visiting this website most definitely NOT because of me, but you’ve probably noticed my fondness of Technoblade as you read browsed along. Perhaps you’re wondering why I admire this person so much. Well, let me share why.

After a very long day back in high school, I would come home completely beat, a little sad, and anxious for the next day of school. But there it was “You’ve got a notification! - Technoblade is live!” I click on the stream and I’m met with this guy playing Minecraft and cracking jokes as TNT drops on him every 10 seconds. All my negative feelings just seemed to disappear.

This has happened more than once, and it’s not just his energy and quirkiness I admired. Techno was just so good at the game, it was pure insanity. You’re just sitting there as he gets jumped 1vs4 and absolutely obliterates each and every single one of his enemies(he only got captured because of his horse Carl). How are you NOT supposed to be impressed?

I’m a Computer Science student and I’m thankful for the opportunity to have used my skills(however limited currently) to build a tribute for such a legend.

Thank you for taking the time to read this short piece of text, and always remember, Technoblade never dies!

Kind regards,
some Techno fan
