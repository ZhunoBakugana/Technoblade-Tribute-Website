The Dark Side of Technoblade

-During Technoblade's Spectacular 20 Million Substravaganza (contains swearing), Mr.Technodad commemorates the Technoblade channel hitting 20mill subscribers on YouTube. Throughout the 3 hour video he shares very silly stories of living with Techno and sarcastically refers to them as “The Dark Side of Technoblade”, which I believe deserve their own little place. Please enjoy :]

-When Techno ran out of toilet paper he would restock from a very specific cabinet and he would never put the toilet paper in the holder. When kindly asked by his father to at least close the cabinet door after he was done using it he would respond with: "Why? I'm just going to open it again next time I need something".

-He wouldn't eat vegetables and his dad would always remind him to. When eating pot pie Techno would always leave out the peas.

-He would bring his used dishes to the dishwasher but never actually put them in.

-When his dad bought some new silverware, Techno hated it for some unknown reason.

-He would do his laundry but wouldn't put it away. So dirty clothes would stack on the ground while clean one clothes would stay in laundry baskets. Over time he would hog all the baskets which meant his family couldn’t use any.

-He would have drinks without using a coster, in the process ruining the finish on the 50$ desk from his dad.

-He would call his siblings "nerds" all the time, unprovoked.
