TECHNOBLADE NEVER DIES!

When Techno was diagnosed with cancer, he didn’t give up. Instead we got to see someone going through hardship while keeping clear sight of who they are, the same old humorous and witty Technoblade. Between recovering from his chemotherapy or surgery, he would make sure to upload videos for us. No matter how time consuming or hard something was, he would conquer without fault.

Whenever you feel lost, sad, anxious, watching a Technoblade video will take off the weight just enough for you to be able to remember “Life isn’t so bad, I got this”. He never acted like he was above his fans. After all he was just some guy playing a block game, that’s how he referred to himself anyways.

He taught us many qualities: resilience, courage, passion, strength, dedication, humility, competitiveness and most importantly, to unapologetically keep being ourselves. He built a community or what some would refer to as a home which is still very much active, that’s the kind of impact Technoblade had on the world.

A few months after his passing, Mr.Technodad established the Technoblade official storefront which would serve as a memorial and as funding for the Sarcoma Foundation of America(SFA). According to BullfrogOne6688 from this thread, all the money generated by Technodad’s merch goes to the foundation and all of the money generated by Technoblade’s merch goes to his family. Sadly, I couldn’t find proof of Mr.Technodad making this statement.

Mr.Technodad with the help of Stacey(his current wife) established the One of Us foundation. Its goal is to give the tools and empowerment to young people to change the world for the better. Similarly to how Technoblade did.
